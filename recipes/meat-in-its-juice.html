Meat In Its Juice

Ingredients:

  • garlic
  • serrano pepper
  • black pepper
  • bouillon, chicken stock, or salt
  • chicken stock or water
  • green onion or spring onion
  • green tomatillos
  • thin-sliced lean beef
  • bacon bits
  • cilantro
  • onion
  • radish
  • lime

Steps:

  1. Render bacon chunks and bacon bits in pot
  2. Grind green tomatillos and serrano pepper into salsa verde, can include garlic in sause.
  3. Cut meat into small strips.
  4. Take bacon out, and lightly fry green onion. Remove green onion shortly.
  5. Add meat, stirring constantly until it gets uniform brown color.
  6. Add salsa verde (and chopped garlic), chicken stock or water, black pepper, bacon bits, and green onions.
  7. Season to preference.
  8. Slow cook for 30-45 minutes.
  9. Chop cilantro, onion, and slice radishes for toppings.
  10. Cut lime in half.
  11. Serve with bacon chunks, toppings, and lime juice.
