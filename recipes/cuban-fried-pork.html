Cuban Fried Pork

Ingredients:

  • pork shoulder/butt
  • salt
  • laurel
  • oregano
  • bitter orange
  • cumin
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • garlic

Steps:

  1. Mix ingredients
  2. Boil ingredients in shallow water
  3. Fry meat on medium heat until golden and fully cooked
