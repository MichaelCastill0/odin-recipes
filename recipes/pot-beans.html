Mexican Pot Beans

Ingredients:

  • pinto beans
  • bouillon or powdered chicken stock
  • oil
  • onion
  • garlic

Steps:

  1. Soak beans overnight.
  2. Boil water.
  3. Drain beans.
  4. Cut onion in half and peel garlic.
  5. Add all ingredients into boiling water, except for salt/bouillon/stock powder.
  6. Add oil.
  7. Once beans are nearly ready, taste test, add seasoning to preference.
