French Toast

Ingredients:

  • bread slices (preferably thick)
  • heavy cream, half-and-half, or whole milk
  • eggs (2 for each cup of cream
  • sugar
  • cinnamon
  • nutmeg
  • butter
  • salt
  • syrup, honey, or agave
  • vanilla (optional)
  • lemon or orange zest(optional)

Steps:

  1. Let bread out to dry overnight. Challah is a good choice.
  2. Make custard with eggs, cream, sugar, pinch of salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and vanilla and/or zest.
  3. Let bread soak on custard for a few minutes for thicker slices, or around 30 seconds for thin slices. Look for a soaked center.
  4. Heat pan to low-mid, and place bread.
  5. Flip when golden and squish to check for uncooked custard before removing.
  6. Serve with syrup, honey, butter, and/or powdered sugar.

Note: For extra richness use egg yolks and heavy cream.
