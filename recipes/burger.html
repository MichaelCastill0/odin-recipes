Burger

Ingredients:

  • 75/25 or 80/20 ground beef
  • thin-sliced onion
  • pickles
  • mustard
  • american cheese
  • butter

Steps:

  1. Toast bread over butter on pan.
  2. Smash patty on high heat.
  3. Top with american cheese after flipping patty.
  4. Caramelize onions, if desired.
  5. Double-stack smashed cheese patties.
  6. Assemble. The. Burger.
