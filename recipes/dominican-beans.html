Dominican Beans

Ingredients:

  • pinto beans
  • pumpkin
  • sweet chili pepper
  • red onion
  • garlic
  • cilantro
  • potato
  • culantro
  • chicken bouillon
  • thyme
  • oregano
  • vinager
  • cooking ham
  • laurel
  • sofrito
  • tomato sauce/paste

Steps:

  1. Soak beans overnight.
  2. Fry ham and sofrito until golden.
  3. Add chopped potato and minced garlic.
  4. Add all other ingredients, but chopped pumpkin, culantro, and cilantro
  5. Let it simmer for a bit, then add pumpkin
  6. Mash some of the beans into a paste and reincorporate, to thicken broth.
  7. Add culantro and cilantro, a couple minutes before the dish is done.
