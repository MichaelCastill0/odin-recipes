French Fries

Ingredients:

  • potates
  • salt

Steps:

  1. Peel potatoes.
  2. Cut into strips of uniform width.
  3. Rinse strips to remove startch.
  4. Blanch in boiling water for 3-5 minutes, depending on size.
  5. Dry and let cool in freezer for at least a few hours.
  6. Fry at 320° for 5-6 minutes.
  7. Remove and let cool.
  8. Fry at 350° for 3-4 mins, or until golden.
  9. Pat excess oil off, and immediately add salt to it.
  10. Shake fries around to distribute salt.
